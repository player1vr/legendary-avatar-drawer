SUMMON SPIRIT

Level 8 Soul Spell · Recall Cost: 2

Once per long rest, spend 2 Hope to summon a Legendary Spirit. Choose a domain other than the Soul domain, shuffle that domain's cards, and draw 5 of them to place beside this card; these become the domain cards the Legendary Spirit can use.

Rule: Each time you make an action roll, or whenever the spirit takes any amount of damage, remove 1 domain card. When all domain cards are removed, the spirit dissipates.
